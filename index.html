Why we’re here

AI is changing the world around us. Young people deserve a chance to shape it.

We want to make AI easier to understand and more exciting to explore—through making, asking questions, and learning together.

✦

From using AI to understanding it

Build confidence with the ideas behind AI, discover what today’s tools can do, and learn where human judgement still matters.

↗

From ideas to useful creations

Turn curiosity into small projects, solve everyday challenges, and share what you learn so others can build on it.

Who you’ll meet

Different ages. Different stories. Room for every curious mind.

We’re creating a place for young people across Malaysia—Chinese, Malay, Indian, and other communities—to learn side by side.

AGES 5–10

Curious explorers

AGES 11–14

Confident experimenters

AGES 15–20

What we could do together

Make learning something you can see, try, and share.

These are proposed activity formats for the community. We’ll publish details after programme plans and dates are confirmed.

01 / EXPLORE🔎

AI discovery labs

Beginner-friendly sessions to explore how AI works, where it appears, and how to use it thoughtfully.

02 / BUILD🛠️

Team build challenges

Work in small teams to design, prototype, and improve an idea that could help someone.

03 / SHARE🎤

Project showcases

Present what you made, hear kind and useful feedback, and inspire someone else’s next idea.

A community built with care

Curiosity needs trust, respect, and good guidance.

We want every young person to feel welcome, respected, and able to learn at a pace that suits them.

1Be kind, respect different views, and give feedback that helps people grow.
2Use AI honestly and thoughtfully. Check important information and protect personal details.
3Participants under 18 should take part with parent or guardian guidance. Participation arrangements will be explained before registration opens.
